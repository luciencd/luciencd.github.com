Practice tips

Play the keyboard

Click or tap a piano key to hear its pitch. Hold a key to sustain it, or press several at once to play a chord.

Finger numbers: 1 thumb · 2 index · 3 middle · 4 ring · 5 pinky. Reverse the fingering when descending. A key may show two numbers in hands-together mode.
